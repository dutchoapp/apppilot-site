AppPilot · Internal tool

Data Deletion Instructions

Effective 18 October 2026

AppPilot is a local single-user application and does not maintain an AppPilot-hosted user account or database. Meta account data used by AppPilot is stored in the operator's local installation.

Revoke Instagram access

Remove AppPilot from the connected Meta/Instagram account's authorized apps, then open AppPilot → Connections → Instagram → Disconnect. This clears the Instagram access/refresh tokens and account identifiers from AppPilot. The Instagram OAuth client credentials and existing AppPilot post records are retained by the current disconnect action.

Erase all AppPilot data on this computer

Stop AppPilot, then remove its local data/rematch.db, data/media/, data/chrome-profile/, and data/secret.key files. Removing the database and key deletes the locally stored connection, post, profile, and usage records; removing the media and Chrome profile deletes generated files and saved browser sessions. This is a full local reset and cannot be undone without a backup.

Content already published

Deleting an AppPilot record does not remove a post already published to Instagram. Delete that post separately in Instagram. Meta's own records and retention are controlled by Meta.

Need help?

Contact support@dutcho.app and identify the service as AppPilot. Do not email passwords, access tokens, or account credentials.
